How the points work

Location scores distance from your map pin. City uses the selected city's center; Country uses that country's representative point. Every round awards up to 5,000 points, and the closest guess scores the most. High total wins after the selected rounds; tied players share the win.

One quick setup for Street View

Add a Mapillary access token on the host device. It is shared with players in each room to load interactive street imagery.
